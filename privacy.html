Privacy Policy

BrightSun Solar App (Demo) · Last updated: 1 November 2026

Demo notice: BrightSun Solar is a fictional business used for a tutorial. When you build your own app, replace the business name, contact details and any wording that does not match what your app really does.

What this app does

The app estimates the size, cost and payback of a rooftop solar system from the electricity bill or units you type in. It also lets you save estimates and contact the business.

Information we collect

Estimates you save. When you tap "Save this estimate", the numbers are stored on your own phone (in the app's local storage). We do not receive them and we cannot see them.

Contact request. If you fill in the "Free site survey" form (name, phone number, area, monthly bill), that information is sent to the business so it can call you back. In the demo, this goes to WhatsApp as a pre-filled message that you choose to send. If the business turns on its online form, the details go to the business's own automation system and are used only to contact you.

What we do not collect. No account, no email address, no location, no contacts, no photos, no advertising ID, and no analytics or tracking tools.

How we use it

Only to give you the estimate and, if you ask, to arrange a site survey and quote. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

Sharing

When you tap "Send on WhatsApp" or "Share", your estimate is passed to WhatsApp or the app you choose. Their privacy policies apply from that point.

Keeping and deleting your data

Saved estimates stay on your phone until you delete them. Use Saved, then "Delete all my saved data" in the app, or clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. To ask us to delete a contact request you sent, write to the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
